News & Newsletter

Bridge Days 2026 — September 25–27, Winterset, Iowa. Our 37th annual gathering. Friday evening welcome supper at the Winterset fire hall, Saturday bridge tours and workshops, Sunday morning business meeting and potluck. Registration ($35 members / $50 non-members) opens in early August. Room blocks at the Village View Motel.

Recent News

Hogback Creek Bridge re-decking complete

July 14, 2026

After six weeks of work, the new white oak decking on our founding bridge is finished. Enormous thanks to Madison County Secondary Roads and to the 41 members who contributed to the decking fund. A reopening ceremony with ribbon cutting is set for Saturday, August 8 at 10:00 a.m. — walkers welcome, dogs on leashes.

Summer issue of The Kingpost in the mail

June 30, 2026

The Summer 2026 issue features a history of the Roann Bridge's 1877 raising, part two of Eleanor Voss's series on reading truss repairs like tree rings, and the full Bridge Days schedule. Members should see it in mailboxes within the week.

Welcome, Ted Wind

June 2, 2026

HCBA is pleased to welcome our summer intern, Ted Wind, who will be leading the slide archive digitization project and assisting with registry updates. Ted has already scanned 400 slides and identified two photographs of bridges we believed undocumented. A full introduction appears in the summer newsletter.

Micro-grant awarded for Roann Bridge signage

May 19, 2026

The board has approved an $1,800 micro-grant to the Friends of the Roann Bridge (Wabash County, Indiana) for interpretive signage at both portals of the 288-foot Howe truss span. Installation is expected before their October festival.

The Kingpost — Our Quarterly Newsletter

The Kingpost has been mailed to members quarterly since 1990 — typed on a Selectric for the first eight years, and only slightly fancier since. Each issue runs twelve pages: bridge histories, restoration updates, member photographs, and the registry-corrections column, “Crossed Timbers.”

Back issues more than two years old are free to download:

  • Spring 2024 — The flood that moved Sandy Creek Bridge (PDF)
  • Winter 2024 — Fire retardants: what we've learned since 2002 (PDF)
  • Fall 2023 — Parke County tour issue (PDF)
  • Summer 2023 — 35 years of HCBA, an oral history (PDF)
