Membership

HCBA runs on dues, small donations, and volunteer hours — in that order of visibility and the reverse order of importance. Membership is open to anyone, anywhere, who thinks a century-old timber bridge is worth keeping dry.

Dues

CategoryAnnual DuesNotes
Student$10With current school enrollment
Individual$25Our most common category
Family / Household$40One newsletter per mailbox
Organizational$100Friends groups, historical societies, county agencies
Lifetime$350One and done; 13 lifetime members to date

What You Receive

  • Four issues a year of The Kingpost, our quarterly newsletter
  • Free fall driving tour maps
  • Member registration rate at Bridge Days
  • Voting rights at the annual business meeting
  • Access to the registry archive by appointment
  • The satisfaction of being one of about ninety people holding up 152 bridges

How to Join

We do things the old-fashioned way: print the membership form, fill it out, and mail it with a check payable to “Heartland Covered Bridge Association” to P.O. Box 217, Winterset, Iowa 50273. You may also pick up a form at the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays.

Download Membership Form (PDF)

Online payment is on our list — the board approved looking into it at the 2025 annual meeting, and our summer intern assures us it can be done.

Volunteer

Not a joiner? We also need bridge photographers, newsletter stuffers (first week of each quarter, coffee provided), Bridge Days help, and people willing to adopt a bridge for an annual condition check. Write to us through the contact page and tell us what you like doing.

Give a Gift

Gift memberships are available in any category — just note the recipient's name and address on the form. We'll send a card announcing your gift.
